Source, limitations and what changed

Based on the supplied CPI Study Support Programme 2026 v4, Module 16, printed pages 497–542. Questions and explanations are newly written revision aids, not official examiner questions or model answers. Coverage spans the module’s major sections but does not reproduce every sentence or replace the pack and its referenced Common Parts material. Page references identify the course source, not independent verification of current law.

The first edition was too brief and sometimes overgeneralised. This version expands proposal content, standard terms, the home, duties, variations, trusts, completion and death. Source contradictions and abbreviated rules are highlighted in Compare terms. Please resolve them with the course tutor and the applicable exam syllabus before memorising an exact rule. This is educational revision, not legal advice.

The 84 recall prompts reuse the quiz bank. Written checklists are self-assessed, not automatic grading. Progress remains in this browser and does not sync. Existing first-edition progress is not deleted, but does not count towards the new question bank.

No accounts, analytics, remote fonts or runtime network requests. Keep this study aid for personal revision; the source study pack is not included in the download.
